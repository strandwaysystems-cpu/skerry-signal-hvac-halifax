Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026. This placeholder privacy policy applies to the HVAC Halifax lead generation website operated by Skerry Signal, a Strandway Ventures company.

Information we collect

When you submit the lead form, we collect the information you choose to provide, including your full name, phone number, email address, Halifax-area property address, service needed, urgency level, referral source, and optional message details.

How we use your information

We use your information to process your HVAC service request, route it through the Skerry Signal lead capture endpoint, and share it with the assigned HVAC contractor so they can contact you about the request.

Sharing

Your information is kept private and shared only with your assigned contractor, service providers used to process the request, and as required by law. We do not publish your personal information.

Consent and communications

By submitting the form, you consent to being contacted by a licensed Halifax HVAC contractor about your request. You may ask us to stop contacting you about a submitted request by emailing founder@skerrysignal.ca.

Data retention

We retain lead information only as long as reasonably necessary to process, track, and manage the service request and related business records.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to founder@skerrysignal.ca.
